01

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Quizaro: AI Quiz Maker ("Quizaro", "we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use the application.

Our goal is to provide useful AI-powered study tools while handling user information responsibly and transparently.

02

Information You Provide

Quizaro allows you to provide study material in order to generate quizzes and related learning content.

This may include:

  • Photos of study pages, notes, or learning material
  • PDF documents
  • Notes or text entered into the app
  • Topics entered for quiz generation
Purpose: This content is used to provide the quiz-generation and learning functionality you request.
03

Artificial Intelligence Processing

Quizaro may use artificial intelligence services to process the study content you submit and generate quiz questions, answers, explanations, or related learning material.

When required to provide these features, submitted content may be transmitted to third-party AI service providers for processing.

Quizaro does not use your submitted study content for targeted advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

04

Photos, PDFs and User Content

User-provided photos, documents, notes, and other study material are processed for app functionality, such as generating quizzes from your content.

We do not sell your submitted study content to data brokers, and we do not use that content to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

05

Accounts and Personal Information

Quizaro does not currently require users to create an account in order to use its core functionality.

The core experience does not require you to provide your name, phone number, or email address.

06

In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Quizaro may offer premium functionality through in-app purchases or auto-renewable subscriptions.

Payments are processed through Apple's App Store payment system.

We do not directly receive or store your full credit card number, bank account information, or Apple Account payment credentials.

07

Advertising and Tracking

Quizaro does not currently use submitted photos, PDFs, notes, or other study material for advertising tracking.

We do not use this content to build advertising profiles or track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.

If future versions introduce advertising or technologies that materially change our privacy practices, this Privacy Policy and our App Store privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.

08

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed through Quizaro.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

09

Children's Privacy

Quizaro is an educational utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected inappropriately, we will take reasonable steps to address the situation.

10

Third-Party Services

Quizaro may rely on third-party providers to support features such as artificial intelligence processing and Apple App Store purchases.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services and may maintain their own privacy policies and data-handling practices.

11

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Quizaro's features, services, legal requirements, or data practices change.

The latest version will be available on this page and will include the most recent update date.

Questions about privacy?

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Quizaro handles information, you can contact the developer directly.
